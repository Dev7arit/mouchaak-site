How a run works

Five steps from brief to shipped. One of them is a person.

Every workflow runs the same way, so you always know where a deliverable is and who has it.

  1. 1

    Brief

    You tell us the outcome, the audience and the constraints. We turn that into a run spec: sources, formats, integrations and the sign-off path.

  2. 2

    Engine run

    The workflow reads everything in scope, works through its fixed steps and produces a complete draft with its sources attached.

  3. 3

    Creative direction

    A strategist or creative director reviews the draft, redirects what needs it and signs off. Nothing ships without this step.

  4. 4

    Ship

    The deliverable lands where it needs to: your CRM, your CMS, your staging site, your inbox.

  5. 5

    Learn

    Results flow back into the run spec, so the next run starts from what worked and skips what didn't.

Who is in the loop

Every account has a named strategist and creative director. They write the brief with you, review every run, and are the people you talk to. The engine does the reading, drafting and checking. They do the directing.

Trust and governance

Built for teams with procurement, legal and a security review.

Enterprise work has rules. The engine was designed around them rather than retrofitted.

  • Isolated workspaces

    Each client runs in its own workspace with its own credentials and storage. Nothing is shared across accounts.

  • Your data stays yours

    Client data is used only to do your work. It is never used to train models, and it is deleted on request.

  • A person signs off

    Every deliverable passes a named reviewer before it ships. Approvals are recorded against the run.

  • Full audit trail

    Every run keeps its sources, drafts and reviewer decisions, so any claim in a deliverable can be traced back.

  • Data residency options

    Processing and storage in India by default, with EU and US options for global teams and regulated sectors.

  • NDA first

    We sign yours or bring ours before any brief, data or credential is shared.

About

A beehive is thousands of specialised workers producing one thing, reliably, around the clock. That is the model: many narrow, well-built workflows, one coordinated output, and a keeper who decides what it is for.

Questions

What people ask before the first run.

What does the engine actually run on?
Frontier reasoning models with very large context windows, orchestrated by workflows we build and maintain. We choose, test and update the underlying models. You get the same workflow, the same reviewer and the same output format regardless of what changes underneath.
Who reviews the work?
A strategist or creative director assigned to your account. They write the brief with you, review every run and sign off before anything ships. You talk to them directly, not through a ticket queue.
Can this work alongside our existing agency?
Yes. Many teams keep their agency for brand and campaign thinking and use Mouchaak for the operational work underneath it. Agencies can also run the engine under their own name through the Enterprise model.
How do you handle our data?
Isolated workspaces per client, no training on client data, deletion on request, and processing in India by default with EU and US options. We sign an NDA before any brief, data or credential is shared, and every run keeps an audit trail.
What happens in a working session?
Forty-five minutes on a call. You bring one real brief. We run it through the relevant workflow while you watch, talk through the output, and give you a turnaround estimate for your actual scope. You keep the output either way.
Do you work with companies outside India?
Yes. We work with teams in India, the US, the UK, Europe and the Middle East. We invoice in INR or USD and keep working hours that overlap with GMT and US Eastern.
How quickly can we start?
A pilot can start within a week of the working session, once the NDA and the run spec are agreed. The first complete run usually ships inside the first two weeks.
